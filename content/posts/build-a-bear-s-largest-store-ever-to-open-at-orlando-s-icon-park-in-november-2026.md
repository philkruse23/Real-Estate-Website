> “Our Icon Park workshop represents our most ambitious experience yet, a place where families can step into the world of Build-A-Bear, share meaningful moments together and create memories that last well beyond their visit.” Chris Hurt, CEO of Build-A-Bear Workshop

Build-A-Bear Workshop is preparing to unveil its largest store ever at **ICON Park in Orlando, Florida, in November 2026**, introducing a three-story flagship destination featuring a rooftop terrace, interactive dessert shop, premium plush design studio, and expanded personalization experiences.

Located at 8431 International Drive, the 33,600-square-foot attraction represents a major expansion for the St. Louis-based retailer. With construction costs estimated at a minimum of $11 million, the project is designed to transform the traditional Build-A-Bear experience into an immersive entertainment destination for families, tourists, and adult fans.

The ambitious development also highlights a growing trend in experiential retail, where businesses are investing in interactive attractions to encourage customer visits and create memorable experiences beyond conventional shopping.

## Inside Build-A-Bear's Largest Flagship Store in Orlando

The new Build-A-Bear flagship will offer visitors an expanded version of the company's signature stuffed-animal customization experience.

Rather than operating solely as a traditional retail location, the Orlando destination will combine shopping, entertainment, food, and personalized activities.

### Premium Design Studio Offers More Customization

One of the flagship's standout features will be a premium Design Studio, offering an elevated version of Build-A-Bear's popular make-your-own plush experience.

Guests will be able to book appointments with design consultants who will guide them through the creation of personalized stuffed animals.

The studio will offer customization options beyond those typically available at standard locations, including the ability to select eye colors and different plush textures.

The appointment-based experience is intended to provide customers with a more exclusive and personalized approach to creating their stuffed animals.

### Interactive Bakeshop Adds a Sweet Experience

The new location will also feature a Bakeshop where visitors can decorate cookies and cakes.

This interactive dessert concept introduces a food-focused activity to the Build-A-Bear experience, giving families an additional reason to spend time at the flagship.

The combination of plush creation and dessert decorating could make the store an appealing stop for tourists looking for hands-on activities along Orlando's International Drive.

### Personalization Station Expands Creative Options

A dedicated personalization station will allow visitors to customize clothing and accessories for their stuffed animals.

Together with the premium Design Studio, this feature will provide customers with more opportunities to create unique plush toys reflecting their individual preferences.

The expanded customization offerings are also consistent with Build-A-Bear's broader strategy of emphasizing personalized merchandise and interactive customer experiences.

### Rooftop Terrace Targets Visitors of All Ages

Perhaps one of the flagship's most distinctive features will be its third-floor rooftop terrace.

The upper level will be partially enclosed, providing an indoor-outdoor environment within the ICON Park entertainment complex.

The rooftop is expected to broaden the store's appeal beyond younger children, particularly as Build-A-Bear continues attracting teenagers and adults.

According to CEO Chris Hurt, nearly 40% of the company's business comes from teens, tweens, and adults.

The rooftop concept could also appeal to visitors attending conventions and events near the Orange County Convention Center.

## Why Orlando's ICON Park Was Selected for the Flagship

Orlando faced competition from other major markets, including Chicago and Las Vegas, before securing the flagship development.

Bobby Palta, principal of BLVD Retail, represented ICON Park in the lease negotiations and previously explained that the opportunity to develop a rooftop terrace near the Orange County Convention Center helped strengthen Orlando's position.

The location places Build-A-Bear within one of Central Florida's busiest tourism corridors, providing access to vacationing families, convention attendees, international tourists, and local residents.

ICON Park is a 20-acre entertainment complex featuring restaurants, retail establishments, and attractions such as Ripley's Crazy Golf and a 500-seat theater for Blue Man Group.

The addition of Build-A-Bear's largest store further strengthens the complex's portfolio of family-oriented entertainment destinations.

ICON Park CEO Chris Jaskiewicz previously expressed appreciation for the company's substantial investment in International Drive.

## Build-A-Bear's $11 Million Investment Expands Its Orlando Presence

Build-A-Bear first announced the ICON Park flagship in March 2025, with construction beginning several months later.

An Orange County building permit indicates that construction costs for the 33,600-square-foot venue are expected to reach at least $11 million.

The flagship will replace Build-A-Bear's existing 1,360-square-foot ICON Park store, which opened in 2021.

At nearly 25 times the size of the original location, the new development represents a dramatic expansion of the retailer's Orlando operations.

The investment also demonstrates continued confidence in International Drive as a destination for tourism-focused retail and entertainment development.

## Experiential Retail Plays a Key Role in Build-A-Bear's Growth Strategy

The November flagship opening comes during a challenging period for Build-A-Bear Workshop.

The company reported total revenue of $115.3 million for the second quarter of fiscal 2026, a 7.2% decline compared with the same period in fiscal 2025.

During an August 27 earnings call, company executives cited macroeconomic conditions and lower store traffic among the factors affecting financial performance.

In response, Build-A-Bear has continued investing in store improvements, personalization options, and enhanced customer experiences.

The ICON Park flagship represents an important part of that strategy.

Hurt indicated that the company intends to use relevant insights and successful concepts from the Orlando location to improve experiences across its broader store portfolio.

This approach reflects an increasingly important trend in the retail industry: transforming physical stores into destinations that provide experiences customers cannot easily replicate through online shopping.

For Build-A-Bear, the combination of premium customization, interactive activities, and entertainment-focused spaces could help generate additional customer interest and encourage longer visits.

However, the financial success of the concept will ultimately depend on whether the expanded experiences translate into stronger customer traffic and spending.

## What Visitors Should Know About the New Orlando Build-A-Bear

Visitors planning a trip to the new flagship can expect the following details:

- **Location:** 8431 International Drive, ICON Park, Orlando, Florida
- **Expected opening:** November 2026
- **Store size:** 33,600 square feet across three stories
- **Main attractions:** Premium Design Studio, Bakeshop, personalization station, and rooftop terrace
- **Design Studio reservations:** Appointments will be required for the premium plush atelier experience
- **Existing location:** The smaller ICON Park store will close when the new flagship opens

An exact opening date and pricing details for the premium experiences have not yet been specified in the announcement.

## A New Chapter for Build-A-Bear and Orlando's Tourism Industry

Build-A-Bear's upcoming ICON Park flagship represents more than an expansion of its retail footprint.

The three-story development introduces a new model for the brand, combining its familiar stuffed-animal customization concept with interactive entertainment, premium design services, and food experiences.

For Orlando, the project adds another major attraction to International Drive while reinforcing the area's appeal to businesses investing in tourism and experiential retail.

For Build-A-Bear, the flagship offers an opportunity to test new concepts that could shape the future of its stores nationwide.

As the November 2026 opening approaches, the development will provide an example of how established retail brands are adapting to changing consumer expectations.

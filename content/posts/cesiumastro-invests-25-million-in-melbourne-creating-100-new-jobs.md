## CesiumAstro Expands Its Space Coast Presence

Founded in 2017, CesiumAstro specializes in satellite communications, advanced antennas, and defense technologies.

The company's new Melbourne facility will focus on developing, integrating, and testing space and defense systems.

With more than 850 employees worldwide, CesiumAstro has rapidly expanded its global operations. Its growth includes the launch of its first operational satellite, Element-1, in October 2026.

The Melbourne investment will provide access to Brevard County's skilled aerospace workforce and established space technology infrastructure.

## Aerospace Jobs Continue to Grow in Brevard County

The Space Coast aerospace and defense industry continues to experience strong hiring demand.

According to Intrepidus Talent Solutions' September 2026 report, 21 tracked employers had **1,844 open positions**, a comparable increase of 9.2% from August.

Blue Origin led the tracked employers with 712 openings, followed by Northrop Grumman with 415 and L3Harris with 344.

The growing number of opportunities highlights demand for engineers, technicians, manufacturing specialists, and other skilled professionals.

## Major Aerospace Investments Across the Space Coast

CesiumAstro's expansion is part of a broader investment trend in Brevard County.

Several major aerospace companies have announced projects, including:

- **Blue Origin:** A planned $600 million manufacturing expansion expected to support 500 jobs.
- **SpaceX:** Expanded facilities and launch infrastructure for Starship operations.
- **Relativity Space:** Additional Terran R manufacturing and launch support facilities.
- **Extant Aerospace:** A $75 million manufacturing project in Melbourne, expected to create 200 jobs.
- **Argotec:** More than $25 million invested in a new Melbourne aerospace facility.

These developments reinforce the Space Coast's growing role in spacecraft manufacturing, satellite technology, and national defense.

## What This Means for Florida's Economy

Continued investments may also encourage workforce development programs and partnerships with educational institutions.

As aerospace companies expand their operations, Brevard County is positioned to benefit from increased business activity and long-term economic opportunities.

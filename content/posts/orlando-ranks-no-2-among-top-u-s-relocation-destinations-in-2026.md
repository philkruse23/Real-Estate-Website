## Florida Dominates the 2026 Relocation Rankings

The PODS Moving Trends Report Pulse Check analyzed long-distance moving inquiries between May and August 2026.

Florida claimed the top three positions, with Sarasota leading the rankings, followed by Orlando and Tampa Bay.

Orlando's strong showing represents a notable change from PODS' earlier annual report, where the city did not appear in the top 10.

However, the reports measure different activities: the annual report tracks completed moves, while the Pulse Check measures moving inquiries.

## Why Are Americans Considering Orlando?

Orlando's popularity reflects broader interest in Florida's lifestyle, employment opportunities, and recreational attractions.

While the PODS report does not identify specific reasons people are considering Orlando, its separate national survey provides insight into relocation decisions.

Among recent movers surveyed:

- **58%** considered affordability a major factor.
- **41%** valued proximity to family and friends.
- **45%** would reject a higher-paying job to remain near their support system.
- **50%** described their move as a fresh start.

These findings suggest Americans increasingly consider overall quality of life alongside career opportunities when choosing where to live.

## What Orlando's Ranking Means for the Housing Market

Growing relocation interest could create opportunities for Orlando's housing and real estate industries.

If inquiries translate into completed moves, increased demand may benefit homebuilders, real estate professionals, rental communities, and local businesses.

However, relocation interest alone does not guarantee population growth or rising home prices.

Housing affordability, insurance costs, employment conditions, and available inventory will continue influencing whether prospective residents make the move.

## Final Thoughts

Orlando's No. 2 ranking highlights Central Florida's continued appeal among Americans exploring new places to live.

With five Florida markets appearing in the national top 10, the Sunshine State remains a prominent destination for potential relocation.

The next question is whether this strong interest will translate into actual moves and sustained housing demand.

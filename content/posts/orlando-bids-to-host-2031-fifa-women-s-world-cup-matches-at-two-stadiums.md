## Orlando Strengthens Its World Cup Bid

Orlando officially submitted its host city proposal on October 14, 2026, through a collaboration involving the Greater Orlando Sports Commission, Orlando City Soccer Club, Orlando International Airport, Visit Orlando, and Orlando Venues.

The proposal included five agreements addressing FIFA's requirements for stadiums, hotels, transportation, training facilities, and host city operations.

Orlando's two-stadium proposal offers flexibility, combining a large multipurpose venue with a dedicated soccer stadium.

## Two Stadiums Could Give Orlando an Advantage

### Camping World Stadium

Camping World Stadium is undergoing approximately $400 million in renovations designed to modernize the facility and improve the experience for major sporting events.

The upgrades could strengthen Orlando's position when FIFA evaluates potential match locations.

### Inter&Co Stadium

Home to Orlando City SC and Orlando Pride, Inter&Co Stadium provides a soccer-specific venue with experience hosting professional and international competitions.

Its established soccer infrastructure adds another advantage to Orlando's proposal.

Together, the stadiums demonstrate the city's ability to accommodate different types of international soccer events.

## Economic Opportunities for Central Florida

Hosting Women's World Cup matches could bring significant benefits to Orlando's tourism and hospitality industries.

International visitors would generate demand for hotels, restaurants, transportation, entertainment, and local attractions.

The 2031 tournament is expected to attract approximately 4.5 million fans across all four host countries, potentially creating substantial economic opportunities for selected cities.

Orlando's experience hosting the 2025 FIFA Club World Cup and other international soccer events could also strengthen its candidacy.

## What's Next for Orlando?

Although FIFA officially awarded the 2031 Women's World Cup to the four host nations on November 23, 2026, Orlando has not yet been confirmed as a match host.

The city must await the final selection of tournament venues.

With upgraded stadium facilities, an established tourism industry, and experience hosting international sporting events, Orlando remains a contender.

## Final Thoughts

Orlando's bid to host the 2031 FIFA Women's World Cup represents another opportunity to expand Central Florida's reputation as a global sports destination.

If selected, the city could welcome thousands of international soccer fans while creating opportunities for local businesses and the tourism economy.

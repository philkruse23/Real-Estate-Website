> “Hotel Beauden reflects where Downtown Orlando is today creative, confident and constantly evolving.”— Benjamin McCarney, General Manager

After 25 years as one of downtown Orlando's most recognizable luxury hotels, the Grand Bohemian Orlando has entered a new chapter. Effective September 1, 2026, the iconic property at 325 S. Orange Avenue officially became **Hotel Beauden (pronounced Boh-den)**.

The transformation goes beyond a name change. It introduces a new restaurant concept, refreshed public spaces, expanded cultural programming, and a renewed focus on Orlando's local identity.

The rebranding also reflects a broader shift in the hospitality industry, where luxury hotels are increasingly emphasizing locally inspired experiences to differentiate themselves and attract both travelers and residents.

## Why Grand Bohemian Orlando Became Hotel Beauden

The Grand Bohemian Orlando opened in 2001 and quickly established itself as a distinctive downtown destination.

Known for its Art Deco-inspired interiors, curated artwork, upscale dining, and live piano performances, the hotel developed a reputation for combining luxury accommodations with cultural experiences.

After more than two decades, owner Xenia Hotels & Resorts moved to reposition the property with an identity more closely connected to the surrounding city.

Hotel Beauden's new branding emphasizes downtown Orlando's creativity, cultural diversity, and evolving hospitality scene.

Rather than focusing solely on traditional luxury accommodations, the hotel aims to create experiences that reflect the character of its local community.

The change also aligns with a growing hospitality trend in which boutique hotels emphasize distinctive local experiences instead of standardized branding.

## What's New at Hotel Beauden Orlando?

### Libra Restaurant Introduces a New Culinary Experience

One of the most significant changes involves the hotel's dining operations.

The former Boheme restaurant and Bösendorfer Lounge have been repositioned under the Libra dining concept, introducing a refreshed menu influenced by Florida's multicultural culinary traditions.

The new direction moves away from the property's previous French-inspired offerings and incorporates Floridian, Hispanic, and Asian influences.

Menu highlights include:

- Smoked crab dip.
- Pork belly with soy glaze, pickled carrots, and toasted cashews.
- Contemporary dishes influenced by regional and international flavors.
- Updated dining options designed to appeal to hotel guests and local residents.

The culinary transformation reflects Orlando's increasingly diverse restaurant landscape and supports Hotel Beauden's effort to connect its hospitality experience with local culture.

### Sunday Jazz Brunch Remains a Signature Tradition

Despite the changes, one of the hotel's best-known dining traditions continues.

The Sunday Jazz Brunch, traditionally held from 10 a.m. to 1:30 p.m., remains a central part of the hotel's dining program.

The experience combines live entertainment, unlimited mimosas, and family-style menu selections.

Maintaining this tradition allows Hotel Beauden to preserve an established connection with longtime guests while introducing its updated identity to new visitors.

Current menus, reservations, and dining details are available through the [official Hotel Beauden website](https://www.hotelbeauden.com/).

### Updated Lobby Artwork and Live Piano Programming

Art and music remain central to the hotel's identity.

As part of the rebranding, the lobby features a new mural with visual references to nearby Lake Eola and downtown Orlando.

The Rotunda, historically known for its live piano performances, has also been refreshed with new tables, seating, and a grand piano.

Additionally, Hotel Beauden announced a partnership with the nonprofit Steinway Society of Central Florida to introduce musical programming within the space.

These improvements are intended to strengthen the hotel's role as a cultural gathering place for visitors and Orlando residents.

## Major Renovations Prepared the Property for Rebranding

The transformation into Hotel Beauden follows several years of reinvestment.

In 2022, The Kessler Collection announced a comprehensive renovation of the Grand Bohemian Orlando, with improvements completed in 2024.

The project included updates to:

- Guest rooms and suites.
- Hotel lobby and common areas.
- Ballrooms and meeting facilities.
- Restaurant and dining spaces.
- Sixth-floor rooftop pool deck.

One of the most notable improvements involved the hotel's elevated pool experience.

Following renovations that expanded public accessibility, General Manager Benjamin McCarney reported that the revamped pool deck contributed approximately **$1 million in additional year-over-year revenue**.

The increase demonstrated the potential value of making hotel amenities accessible to a broader audience beyond overnight guests.

The improvements also helped position the property for its transition to Hotel Beauden.

## Xenia Hotels & Resorts Introduces New Hotel Management

The rebranding includes an important operational transition.

Orlando-based real estate investment trust Xenia Hotels & Resorts (NYSE: XHR) continues to own the property.

However, Atlanta-based Davidson Hospitality Group has taken over hotel management responsibilities from The Kessler Collection.

The Kessler Collection originally developed the Grand Bohemian Orlando and operated the property for many years.

In 2012, an entity associated with Xenia Hotels & Resorts acquired the property for approximately $77 million, according to Orange County records.

During Xenia's second-quarter 2026 earnings call, President and Chief Operating Officer Barry Bloom outlined plans to reposition four Marriott Autograph Collection hotels previously operating under the Grand Bohemian name.

The properties include hotels in:

- Orlando, Florida.
- Savannah, Georgia.
- Charleston, South Carolina.
- Mountain Brook, Alabama.

According to Bloom, the new branding strategy is intended to give each hotel a distinctive identity reflecting its local market.

Davidson Hospitality Group was also selected to manage all four properties.

Despite these changes, Hotel Beauden continues to operate within Marriott's Autograph Collection, maintaining its connection to an established international hospitality network.

## What Hotel Beauden's Rebranding Means for Downtown Orlando

Hotel Beauden's transformation comes as downtown Orlando continues to evolve as a center for business, entertainment, dining, and cultural experiences.

While the city's tourism industry is often associated with major theme parks, downtown Orlando serves a different market that includes corporate travelers, convention attendees, event visitors, and local residents.

Luxury hotels play an important role in supporting these activities by providing accommodations, restaurants, meeting facilities, and event spaces.

According to the Downtown Orlando Community Redevelopment Agency's fourth-quarter 2025 market report, the downtown hotel market recorded several notable indicators:

| Hotel Market Indicator | Q4 2025 |
| --- | --- |
| Hotel room inventory | 2,215 rooms |
| Occupancy rate | 70.4% |
| Year-over-year occupancy increase | 2.9% |
| Average daily room rate | $180.64 |
| Orlando citywide average daily rate | $173.67 |

These figures highlight the importance of downtown Orlando's hospitality market.

By expanding its focus on dining, entertainment, arts, and cultural programming, Hotel Beauden is positioning itself to serve both overnight visitors and the local community.

The hotel's strategy also reflects a broader industry movement toward experiential hospitality, where unique dining options, distinctive design, and neighborhood connections help properties compete.

## Hotel Beauden Maintains Its Luxury Boutique Identity

Although its name and branding have changed, Hotel Beauden continues to offer several features associated with the former Grand Bohemian Orlando.

The property remains a 247-room boutique luxury hotel located in the heart of downtown Orlando.

Its proximity to downtown attractions, including the Dr. Phillips Center for the Performing Arts and Lake Eola Park, provides convenient access to entertainment, dining, and recreational activities.

Hotel Beauden also continues to provide spaces for weddings, corporate meetings, social gatherings, and special events.

The combination of updated facilities, Marriott affiliation, and locally focused experiences supports the hotel's positioning within Orlando's competitive hospitality market.

## A New Chapter for a Downtown Orlando Landmark

The transition from Grand Bohemian Orlando to Hotel Beauden marks a significant milestone for one of the city's longstanding hospitality properties.

After 25 years, the hotel is moving forward with a new name, updated dining concepts, refreshed cultural spaces, and a different management team.

At the same time, many of the characteristics that established its reputation, including art, music, upscale accommodations, and signature dining experiences, remain part of its identity.

The rebranding illustrates how established luxury hotels can evolve alongside the communities they serve while preserving elements of their original appeal.

For downtown Orlando, Hotel Beauden represents another investment in the area's hospitality and cultural landscape.

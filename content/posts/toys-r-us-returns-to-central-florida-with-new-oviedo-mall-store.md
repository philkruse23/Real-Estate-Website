## Toys “R” Us Makes Its Central Florida Comeback

The new Toys “R” Us location occupies approximately 5,200 square feet in Oviedo Mall's west wing, replacing the former Champs Sports store.

Announced in September 2026, the location marks the brand's return to standalone retail in the area following its bankruptcy and liquidation in 2018.

The store is expected to feature popular toys, games, collectibles, and gifts, appealing to children and adults alike.

The opening is part of a larger expansion through a partnership with Texas-based Go! Retail Group, which announced plans for 120 new standalone Toys “R” Us locations nationwide ahead of the holiday season.

## Adult Collectors Drive Toy Industry Growth

The comeback coincides with rising demand for toys among adult consumers.

According to [Circana](https://www.circana.com/post/us-toy-industry-posts-strongest-first-half-in-six-years-circana-reports), U.S. toy sales increased 17% during the first half of 2026 compared with the same period in 2025. Sales to adults ages 18 and older jumped 25%.

Popular products such as LEGO sets, trading cards, action figures, and collectibles have helped transform toys into hobbies and social experiences.

This growing audience presents new opportunities for Toys “R” Us beyond traditional children's merchandise.

## Why Oviedo Mall Is a Promising Location

Oviedo Mall has built a reputation for hosting unconventional community events, including comic gatherings, book festivals, and goblin markets.

Its emphasis on entertainment and community engagement aligns with the growing popularity of collectibles and hobby-focused shopping.

The arrival of Toys “R” Us could help attract families, collectors, and nostalgic shoppers while supporting the mall's efforts to become more than a traditional retail destination.

## A New Chapter for Toys “R” Us

The return of Toys “R” Us reflects changing consumer preferences and the continuing appeal of physical shopping experiences.

Although nostalgia may initially attract customers, the retailer's long-term success will depend on its merchandise selection, pricing, and ability to encourage repeat visits.

For Central Florida, the Oviedo Mall location represents an opportunity to reconnect with an iconic brand while introducing a new generation to the excitement of toy shopping.

**Will Toys “R” Us succeed in its Central Florida comeback?** Its performance at Oviedo Mall could offer valuable insight into the future of nostalgic brands and experiential retail.

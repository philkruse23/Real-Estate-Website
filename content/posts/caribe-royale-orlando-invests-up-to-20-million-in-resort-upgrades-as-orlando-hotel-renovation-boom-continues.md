Located near Walt Disney World, the 1,337-room resort is positioning itself for continued growth in Orlando's leisure travel and convention markets.

## Caribe Royale Orlando Announces Major Resort Improvements

Caribe Royale Orlando, located at 8101 World Center Drive, has announced plans to invest up to $20 million in improvements designed to enhance the overall guest experience.

The projects focus on dining, accommodations, and entertainment, three important components of the modern resort experience.

According to Amaury Piedra, vice president of operations and managing director of Caribe Royale Orlando, the improvements reflect the resort's commitment to long-term growth.

“As Caribe Royale Orlando enters its fourth decade, the resort remains focused on thoughtful growth, continual reinvestment in both our guests and staff development, and delivering exceptional hospitality for leisure travelers, meeting planners and event attendees,” Piedra said.

### The Venetian Chop House Receives a Dining Room Redesign

The Venetian Chop House, Caribe Royale's signature Italian steakhouse, is undergoing a dining room redesign scheduled for unveiling in October 2026.

Known for its premium steaks, fresh seafood, extensive wine selection, and craft cocktails, the restaurant is a key component of the resort's food-and-beverage offerings.

The redesigned space is intended to enhance the dining environment for leisure travelers, convention attendees, and other resort guests.

Restaurant renovations can also create additional revenue opportunities by encouraging guests to dine on property rather than visiting off-site establishments.

### Resort Refreshes 120 Two-Bedroom Villas

Caribe Royale is also updating its 120 two-bedroom villas, with the renovated accommodations scheduled to debut in phases beginning in October 2026.

The improvements are designed to provide a refreshed lodging experience for families, groups, and travelers seeking additional space.

Beginning in early 2027, villa guests are also expected to have access to a dedicated concierge service.

The addition of personalized services reflects the growing emphasis on differentiated hospitality experiences, particularly within upscale resort properties.

By combining upgraded accommodations with exclusive guest services, the resort aims to strengthen its appeal in Orlando's competitive hotel market.

### Stadium Club Expands With New Tailgate Experience

The resort's Stadium Club sports entertainment venue is also scheduled for expansion.

Opened in January 2024, Stadium Club currently spans more than 9,000 square feet and features sports simulation suites, dozens of televisions, and elevated bar food.

The expansion will introduce a new experience called **Tailgate**, which is expected to open by late 2027.

The addition supports the growing role of entertainment attractions within large resort properties.

Sports-themed entertainment venues can attract both overnight guests and local visitors while creating additional opportunities for food, beverage, and event revenue.

## New Investment Follows $160 Million Resort Transformation

The latest announcement comes less than a year after Caribe Royale completed its extensive $160 million renovation program in December 2025.

Launched in 2020, the multiyear transformation involved major improvements across the property, including refreshed guest rooms, an updated lobby, and expanded meeting facilities.

The resort now offers more than 260,000 square feet of meeting space, making it Orlando's sixth-largest convention hotel, according to Orlando Business Journal research.

The additional $18 million to $20 million investment signals that the property's improvement strategy extends beyond periodic large-scale renovations.

Instead, ongoing reinvestment is becoming a central part of maintaining its position in the leisure and convention hospitality markets.

## Orlando's Strong Convention Market Supports Hotel Investment

Convention and meetings business continues to play an important role in Caribe Royale's performance.

Piedra previously reported that the resort experienced stronger business during the first half of 2026 compared with the same period in 2025.

Much of that momentum came from increased convention demand.

"What has stood out most is the strength of demand for meetings in 2027 and 2028," Piedra said.

He noted that organizations were actively planning meetings well in advance, with significant interest converting into confirmed business during the first half of 2026.

The demand highlights the importance of high-quality meeting facilities and comprehensive resort amenities.

Properties capable of combining modern conference spaces with upgraded accommodations, dining, and entertainment offerings may be better positioned to attract corporate meetings, conventions, and large-scale events.

For Orlando, continued interest from meeting planners also provides an important source of tourism activity beyond the city's traditional theme park visitation.

## Orlando Resorts Invest Millions in Property Renovations

Caribe Royale's announcement is part of a larger wave of hospitality reinvestment throughout Central Florida.

Several major resort operators have announced substantial renovation programs aimed at improving their properties and strengthening long-term competitiveness.

### Rosen Hotels & Resorts Plans More Than $150 Million in Upgrades

In August 2026, Rosen Hotels & Resorts announced plans to invest more than $150 million in renovations at two of its flagship Orlando properties, Rosen Plaza and Rosen Shingle Creek.

The program represents the largest investment in the company's 52-year history.

Planned improvements include redesigned guest rooms and upgraded meeting spaces.

The investment underscores the importance of maintaining modern facilities in a market where competition for convention and leisure travelers remains significant.

### Disney's Animal Kingdom Lodge Undergoes Multimillion-Dollar Improvements

Walt Disney World is also investing in renovations at Disney's Animal Kingdom Lodge.

Records obtained by the Orlando Business Journal from the Central Florida Tourism Oversight District indicate that at least $24.4 million is being invested in the project.

The work includes improvements to guest rooms and common areas.

Although Disney's resort properties serve a different segment of the market from large convention hotels, the investments reflect a similar emphasis on maintaining guest satisfaction and property quality.

## U.S. Hotel Renovation Activity Continues to Grow

Hotel renovation activity is increasing beyond Orlando.

According to the **Q2 2026 Construction Pipeline Trend Report from Lodging Econometrics**, hotel conversions and renovation projects across the United States increased 7% compared with the second quarter of 2025.

The trend suggests that more property owners are prioritizing improvements to existing hotel assets.

PCL Construction Services Inc., one of Central Florida's largest general contractors, identified similar conditions in its 2026 State of Hospitality Construction report.

The company reported increasing interest among hotel owners in property upgrades and renovations as they respond to changing guest expectations and competitive pressures.

Charlie Dorr, director of business development for PCL's Florida Buildings operations, emphasized the ongoing nature of hotel reinvestment.

“Every few years, properties need to be refreshed to stay competitive. That cycle never really stops,” Dorr said.

The continued renovation cycle may provide construction opportunities for general contractors, specialty trades, engineering firms, architects, designers, and hospitality suppliers.

## What Hotel Renovations Mean for Orlando's Economy

Major resort renovation projects can contribute to Central Florida's economy through construction spending, supplier activity, and ongoing hospitality operations.

While new hotel development increases room inventory, renovations focus on improving the performance and competitiveness of existing properties.

These projects can involve a wide range of services, including:

- Interior construction and architectural improvements.
- Mechanical, electrical, and plumbing upgrades.
- Furniture, fixtures, and equipment installation.
- Restaurant and entertainment venue improvements.
- Technology and guest service enhancements.

Hospitality renovations also present operational challenges because many projects must be completed while resorts continue serving guests.

Careful scheduling and coordination are necessary to minimize disruptions, particularly at properties with active convention and group business.

For Central Florida's construction sector, the growing number of resort improvement programs represents a potentially valuable source of project opportunities.

However, the overall economic impact will depend on project spending, labor demand, procurement decisions, and the financial performance of the upgraded properties.

## Guest Experiences Drive the Next Phase of Resort Competition

The hospitality industry's emphasis on guest experiences is becoming increasingly evident in Orlando's latest resort investments.

While location, room quality, and pricing remain important, hotels are also differentiating themselves through dining, entertainment, personalized services, and enhanced meeting environments.

Caribe Royale's planned improvements illustrate this strategy.

The Venetian Chop House redesign strengthens the resort's dining experience, the villa renovations enhance premium accommodation options, and the Stadium Club expansion introduces additional entertainment opportunities.

Together, these projects are intended to create a more comprehensive resort experience while generating new opportunities for guest spending.

The strategy may also help properties appeal to multiple customer segments, including families, leisure travelers, corporate groups, convention planners, and local visitors.

Still, continued capital investment does not automatically guarantee improved financial performance.

Long-term results will depend on factors such as room rates, occupancy levels, operating expenses, customer satisfaction, and returns on renovation spending.

## Final Thoughts: Orlando's Hospitality Investment Cycle Shows Continued Momentum

Caribe Royale Orlando's latest $18 million to $20 million improvement program highlights the importance of continued reinvestment in Central Florida's hospitality industry.

Following the completion of its $160 million transformation, the resort is moving forward with additional projects designed to enhance guest experiences and strengthen its competitive position.

Similar investments by Rosen Hotels & Resorts and Walt Disney World demonstrate that established Orlando properties are prioritizing upgrades as the hospitality market evolves.

With convention demand remaining strong and hotel renovation activity increasing nationally, the region's construction and hospitality industries could continue benefiting from this investment cycle.

The long-term impact will become clearer as projects are completed and operators evaluate their performance.

**For hospitality leaders, property owners, developers, and construction professionals, these projects highlight the importance of monitoring changing guest expectations and identifying opportunities for strategic reinvestment.**

As Orlando's resort renovation activity continues, the industry's ability to balance capital improvements with measurable business results will remain an important factor in sustaining competitiveness.

Meet Phil Kruse — Your Orlando Expert

Lakefront and luxury real estate, done by someone who's actually from here.

Phil Kruse has called Orlando home since 2001, and he'll tell you straight: it's the greatest city in the United States. As a lakefront and luxury real estate specialist with over a decade of experience, Phil has built his career on a simple belief — if you don't call Orlando home yet, you should. And if you already do, you deserve an expert who knows every lake, every neighborhood, and every hidden gem this city has to offer.

Phil specializes in helping buyers and sellers navigate Orlando's lakefront and luxury markets, bringing local insight you won't find anywhere else. But his commitment to this city goes beyond real estate. A portion of every commission is donated to the charity of your choice — or to the New Image Youth Center, where Phil volunteers every Wednesday during the school year, teaching improv comedy to local kids.

When you work with Phil, you're not just hiring an agent. You're partnering with someone who genuinely believes in Orlando and is invested in making it better, one family and one lake at a time.
